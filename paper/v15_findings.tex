\section{Interpretation and Limitations}
The contrast isolates the reward package conditional on V2. It does not identify
individual terms or behavior without the navigation prior and prospective
filter. These components may compensate for reward deficiencies. Removing them
would require a separate experiment and fresh evaluation.

Original speed limits, motion, discrete action parameterization, continued
training after arrival and unknown implementation details differ. Reconstructing
the reward on V2 cannot identify the cause of the thesis's wandering
\cite[p.~21, Sec.~8]{thesis}. Exact dataset version and sentinel meaning remain
unverified. One map, static load, ideal service and uncalibrated energy limit
inference. No physical continuity, packet latency, collision safety or deployment
claim follows. A successful arrival can still retain queued data. Every table
here uses the newly declared route seeds; earlier V2 results remain separate.

\section{Conclusion}
\ConclusionResult{} The experiment compares the written original reward and a
replacement under identical sampled constraints and control machinery, retaining
failed missions and communication tradeoffs. Further claims require independent
maps, traffic and controller ablations rather than tuning on these test routes.
