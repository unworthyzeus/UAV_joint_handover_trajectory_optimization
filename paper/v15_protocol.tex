\section{Original Reward Intervention and Experimental Protocol}
\subsection{V1.5: Original Written Reward on V2}
For executed postfilter delay $D$, interference $I$ and handover $h$, use
\begin{align}
r_{\rm orig}={}&\frac{0.35}{1+10D}+\frac{0.30}{1+10^5I}
 +\frac{0.35}{1+100h}\nonumber\\
 &+12\mathbb{1}[d_{t+1}<d_t]-10b-10o-10s.
\end{align}
The source supplies the algebra, equal weights and coefficients
\cite[p.~12, Eqs.~(13)--(14); p.~15, Table~3]{thesis}. Here $b$ is a boundary
violation, $o$ is RSS $\leq-96$ dBm, and $s$ is speed $\geq100$ m/s.
Energy exhaustion replaces the whole reward with $-10$
\cite[p.~12, Sec.~5.2]{thesis}. No new arrival, timeout or overflow payment
is added. The source's removed energy utility and data dump penalty remain
absent\cite[pp.~20--21, Sec.~7.3]{thesis}.

The reward retains its RSS equality penalty although the joint constraint
permits equality. Its speed threshold remains 100 m/s, unreachable under the
V2 25 m/s cap. Substituting 25 m/s would invent a penalty on routine cruising.
The same V2 transition supplies radio inputs, physics, filter interventions
and terminal outcomes to all arms. The wrapper changes only returned reward
and accumulated raw return. Full reward replacement changes the entire package;
the effects of shaping, terminal payments and removing positive per step
utilities are not separately identified.

\subsection{Shared Training and Fixed Checkpoints}
PPO uses separate two layer 64 unit tanh actor and critic networks, 64 lanes,
128 step rollouts, four epochs, minibatches of 512, learning rate
$3\times10^{-4}$, Adam epsilon $10^{-5}$, discount 0.99, GAE 0.95, clip 0.2,
entropy weight 0.005, value weight 0.5 and gradient norm limit 0.5.
Two CPU threads are used. Running discounted return variance normalizes rewards,
clipped to $[-10,10]$. This shared processing responds to changed raw rewards;
potential algebra alone does not establish invariance after these transforms.
The source uses Stable Baselines PPO\cite[p.~13, Sec.~5.3]{thesis} and different
training settings\cite[pp.~15--16, Sec.~6.2 and Table~4]{thesis}.

All arms use the exact 4,096 V2 training routes spanning 200--1800 m, 64 standard
validation routes and 32 longer validation routes. Five initialization seeds,
2101--2105, and 524,288 interactions per policy are shared. Five original reward
policies are newly trained from scratch: 2,621,440 new interactions. All ten
completed full and arrival V2 policies are reused; they used identical training
data, seeds and budget. Their hashes were fixed before new training and test
evaluation. No seed is selected for its performance. Different policies can
reset at different times, so equal seeds and budgets do not imply identical
realized transitions. Every run uses its final budget checkpoint.

Earlier V2 test outcomes were known. This protocol was frozen before the new
training and fresh test evaluation; it is not independently registered or
claimed to predate the earlier V2 study. No new hyperparameter tuning or policy
selection uses the fresh tests.

\subsection{Fresh Tests and Prespecified Analysis}
Generate 200 standard routes of 200--1000 m using seed 53012 and 200 longer
routes of 1000--1800 m using seed 53013. Exact route pairs are disjoint from
all prior saved routes; every controller shares initial conditions and load
phases. Both lengths occur in training. This is route holdout on one map,
not geographic generalization or length extrapolation. The source does not
document equivalent independent splits\cite[pp.~15--16, Secs.~6.2--6.3]{thesis}.

The primary contrast is full minus original standard joint success. Use 5,000
crossed seed and route bootstrap draws, seed 63000, to estimate a 95\% interval.
An interval entirely above zero supports improved primary completion under
this design. Longer completion is prespecified secondary. Five seeds on 200
shared routes are not 1,000 independent training replications. Secondary
intervals are descriptive and unadjusted for multiple comparisons.

Secondary metrics use the common successful seed/route pairs, with their count
visible. Unconditional success and failure rates remain separate. No percentage
is computed for logarithmic SINR. Three learned arms supply 6,000 episodes;
four deterministic references supply 1,600, totaling 7,600 new final evaluations.
These records are separate from the previously evaluated V2 routes.

\subsection{Deterministic References and Verification}
Goal RSS uses nominal braking motion and strongest admissible association.
Goal radio uses the same motion and prospective normalized radio cost selection.
One step and three step joint search examine nine initial motion primitives,
varying direction and speed. The three step version follows each initial
choice with two predicted reference steps. All share the same safety filter,
which can alter nominally straight paths. These local methods can fail and
are not a reproduction of the source greedy controller
\cite[pp.~13--14, Sec.~5.4]{thesis}.

All \TestCount\ tests pass, including unchanged states, observations, masks
and terminal records under identical actions across reward arms. Failure checks
cover RSS, overflow, boundary, energy and timeout as well as arrival. A syntax
comparison verifies that copied PPO differs only in its environment import
and module description. Small full reward training reproduces frozen V2
parameters exactly; original reward training repeats exactly in its deterministic
check. All \ReplayCount\ new final episode records replayed exactly. Source,
dataset and checkpoint hashes remain unchanged. These checks verify software
reproduction, not physical model validity.
